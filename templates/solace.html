SOLACE 01 — Copenhagen

Every other pair fights the room by shouting over it. We would rather the room simply stopped.

Noise cancellation is not a feature we added at the end. It is the reason the rest of the object is shaped the way it is. The cups are sealed before they are styled. The clamp is measured in grams before it is measured in taste. Nine parts, because every seam is a way in for the world.

What is left is not a headphone that plays music loudly. It is a headphone that removes everything that was ever in the way of it — and then gets out of the way itself. You stop noticing the product roughly four seconds after you put it on. That is the entire design brief.
