Ask a question
How does the cash back work?

When you buy with Dwella, part of the buyer’s agent commission is credited back to you at closing. It appears as a line on your closing statement, usually as a credit toward your closing costs.

Is the cash back taxed?

In most cases a buyer rebate is treated as a reduction in the price of the home, not as income. Your situation may differ, so check with a tax adviser.

Do I have to use a Dwella mortgage?

No. You can bring any lender you like and still get your commission back. The extra $3,000 closing credit only applies when you finance with Dwella.

Where does Dwella work?

We’re live in 32 states and Washington, D.C. A few states don’t allow rebates; there we lower your costs another way, such as covering your inspection.

How fast can I close?

Our buyers close in 21 days on average from an accepted offer. Search, loan and title run on one timeline, so nothing waits on a handoff.

What does it cost to use Dwella?

Searching, saving homes, tours and talking to an agent are free. We’re paid from the commission at closing, the same way any agent is.
