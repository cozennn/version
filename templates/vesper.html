Scroll to set the sun

Est. 2011 — Porto & Lisbon

A house is a machine for holding a day. We build the machine around the hour you actually care about.

Every VESPER project starts with a year of sun studies before a single wall is drawn. We model the site through all four solstices, find the hour the client keeps describing without naming, and put the building's whole weight behind it.

The result is architecture that is deliberately unremarkable at noon. It is not built for the photograph. It is built for 19:05, when the concrete goes soft and the glass gives up and everything inside turns amber.
